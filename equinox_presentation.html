Equinox Membership Tier Redesign

Key Objectives

Define the future role of core membership, and how products, services, and partnerships support it+
Not just repricing existing tiers, defining what the membership itself is for.
Build a consumer-backed view of what current and prospective members value most+
Grounded in Equinox's own data first, net-new research only where a real gap exists.
Recommend an architecture that's economically attractive, brand-aligned, and operationally feasible+
Three tests, not one. A recommendation only clears the bar if it passes all three.
Outline a path into a pilot, so Equinox can move quickly once the direction is set+
Where Design concludes a pilot is warranted, not by default.

Current State

Tiers are defined by physical access, not by the breadth of lifestyle benefits the ecosystem delivers+
PT, Pilates, spa, retail, Optimize, Circle, all real, all currently separate.
Leads up double digits; closing rate negative every quarter of 2025+
3,800 net members lost in Q4 alone, the worst quarter of the year.
Rate increases have been smallest where the member base is largest+
~3% on Select/Regional vs. 11.3% on All Access for 2026.
Member expectations are shaped by luxury hospitality and curated retail, not other gyms+
Aman, Amex Centurion, Soho House, the comp set members already use.

Opportunity

Define what members value most, and how that varies by segment and use case+
Individual, family, corporate, by market, by tier, by partner.
Increase acquisition, retention, engagement, and revenue through one architecture+
Mix-shift compounds the way Costco's Executive tier already proves it can.
Build something that can flex as new data, partnerships, and products come in+
A hypothesis to test, not a finished blueprint.

Design Principles

1. Continuity+
Protect the member we have while building for the member we want. Migration considerations designed in from the start.
2. Simplicity+
If club staff can't explain it in one sentence, it isn't ready.
3. Design for Reality+
Design for how people actually join and use the membership, individual, family, corporate, partner, and why they churn.
4. Near-term Impact+
Show progress before the big reveal. Quick wins and validation along the way, and manage for acquisition and retention.
5. Value Creation+
Every benefit earns its place economically. New or trendy isn't a reason to add something to a tier; it needs a clear member value and a clear cost that justifies it.
