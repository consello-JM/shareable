Equinox Membership Tier Redesign

Key Objectives

Define the future role of core membership, and how products, services, and partnerships support it+
Demonstrate the power of an Equinox Membership to deliver disproportionate value for money regardless of where members are on their journey.
Build a consumer-backed view of what current and target prospective members value most+
Grounded in Equinox's own data first, with net-new research only where a real gap exists.
Recommend an architecture that's economically attractive, brand-aligned, member-centric, and operationally feasible+
We won't recommend anything we haven't checked against real people, the numbers, the brand, and what clubs can actually run.
Outline a path into a pilot, so Equinox can move quickly once the direction is set+
If a pilot isn't viable, we validate through quick wins and iterative testing instead.

Current State

Tiers are defined by physical access, narrower than the breadth of lifestyle benefits the ecosystem delivers+
PT, Pilates, spa, retail, Optimize, and Circle are all real and all currently separate. The same access framing likely confuses prospective members too; evaluating a tier by which building they can enter doesn't tell them what they're actually signing up for, which is a plausible contributor to the closing-rate problem.
Leads up double digits; closing rate negative every quarter of 2025+
Real demand walks away because the value isn't clear enough to convert.
Rate increases have been smallest where the member base is largest+
~3% on Select/Regional vs. 11.3% on All Access for 2026.
Member expectations are increasingly shaped by luxury hospitality and curated retail+
Members already benchmark Equinox against Aman and Soho House, standards the current club-access tiers weren't designed to answer.

Opportunity

Define what members value most, and how that varies by segment and use case+
By individual, family, corporate, market, tier, or partner.
Increase acquisition, retention, engagement, and revenue through one architecture+
Create a meaningful membership experience with benefits, support, and programming so enticing it would be unthinkable to leave.
Build something that can flex as new data, partnerships, and products come in+
A hypothesis to test as the architecture takes shape, will this still work in 5 years? 10 years?

Process

Five workstreams across three phases

Discover (~6 weeks)+
Benchmarks Equinox against adjacent luxury and lifestyle categories to identify transferable tier, access, and loyalty practices informed by high-net-worth spending patterns.

Builds a consumer-backed view of members through segmentation, personas, and willingness-to-pay analysis across key lifestyle categories.
Design (~8 weeks)+
Builds a tier framework rooted in lifestyle affinity, mapping benefits across the Equinox ecosystem into clear upgrade pathways and a value-based pricing model benchmarked against luxury peers.

Redesigns the promotional approach and defines a policy framework supported by revenue and LTV scenario analysis.

Any new products or partnerships are evaluated through this same tier framework, prioritized as benefits rather than standalone offerings, and filtered for brand fit and operational feasibility.
Roadmap (~3 weeks)+
Defines the rollout plan and a member migration playbook to transition existing members into the new architecture, paired with a brand-aligned communications strategy.

Experience KPIs are established to track performance against the redesign's goals.
Implementation+
We work alongside your team, including your field organization, to successfully execute the plan in the market.
Accelerated Path+
Equinox can accelerate the timeline in two ways and can choose between them at different points in the project.

Design Principles

1. Continuity+
Protect the member we have while building for the member we want. Migration considerations designed in from the start.
2. Simplicity+
It's ready once Membership Advisors can explain it in one sentence.
3. Design for Reality+
Design for how people actually join and use the membership, as an individual, family, corporate, partner, and why they churn.
4. Near-term Impact+
Show progress before the big reveal.

Share quick wins and validation along the way, and manage for acquisition and retention.
5. Value Creation+
Every benefit earns its place economically: a clear member value and a clear cost justify it, regardless of how new or trendy it is.

Recruit, retain, and elevate the brand framework.

The Diagnosis

Real assets, disconnected from each other+
