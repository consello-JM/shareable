Equinox Membership Tier Redesign

Key Objectives

Define the future role of core membership, and how products, services, and partnerships support it+
Demonstrate the power of an Equinox Membership to deliver disproportionate value for money regardless of where members are on their journey.
Build a consumer-backed view of what current and target prospective members value most+
Grounded in Equinox's own data first, net-new research only where a real gap exists.
Recommend an architecture that's economically attractive, brand-aligned, member-centric, and operationally feasible+
We won't recommend anything we haven't checked against the numbers, the brand, and what clubs can actually run.
Outline a path into a pilot, so Equinox can move quickly once the direction is set+
If a pilot isn't viable, we validate through quick wins and iterative testing instead.

Current State

Tiers are defined by physical access, narrower than the breadth of lifestyle benefits the ecosystem delivers+
PT, Pilates, spa, retail, Optimize, Circle, all real, all currently separate. The same access framing likely confuses prospective members too, evaluating a tier by which building they can enter doesn't tell them what they're actually signing up for, a plausible contributor to the closing-rate problem.
Leads up double digits; closing rate negative every quarter of 2025+
That's real demand walking away, not because the brand doesn't resonate, but because the value isn't clear enough to convert.
Rate increases have been smallest where the member base is largest+
~3% on Select/Regional vs. 11.3% on All Access for 2026.
Member expectations are increasingly shaped by luxury hospitality and curated retail+
Members already benchmark Equinox against Aman and Soho House, standards the current club-access tiers weren't designed to answer.

Opportunity

Define what members value most, and how that varies by segment and use case+
Individual, family, corporate, by market, by tier, by partner.
Increase acquisition, retention, engagement, and revenue through one architecture+
Create a meaningful membership experience with benefits, support, and programming so enticing it would be unthinkable to leave.
Build something that can flex as new data, partnerships, and products come in+
A hypothesis to test as the architecture takes shape.

Implementation+

Accelerated Path+

Design Principles

1. Continuity+
Protect the member we have while building for the member we want. Migration considerations designed in from the start.
2. Simplicity+
It's ready once Membership Advisors can explain it in one sentence.
3. Design for Reality+
Design for how people actually join and use the membership, as an individual, family, corporate, partner, and why they churn.
4. Near-term Impact+
Show progress before the big reveal. Quick wins and validation along the way, and manage for acquisition and retention.
5. Value Creation+
Every benefit earns its place economically: a clear member value and a clear cost justify it, regardless of how new or trendy it is. Recruit, Retain, Elevate the Brand framework.

Discovery through Execution

1

The Diagnosis

High Level, requires validation
Funnel: leads up, closing rate negative all of 2025+
3,800 net members lost in Q4 alone, the worst quarter of the year.
Margin: smallest increases where the base is largest+
~3% on Select/Regional vs. 11.3% on All Access for 2026. Tier mix essentially flat since 2019.
Real assets, disconnected from each other+
PT, Pilates, spa, retail, Optimize, Circle. Each performs alone.
The tier names don't hold up under scrutiny+
All Access doesn't mean all access. Newer amenities land in whichever tier is newest, not the one members would value.
2

Our Approach

Phases and gates
Discover → Design → Roadmap+
~16–18 weeks. Three priced phases, not a menu.
Five gates along the way+
Not a fixed handoff at the end. Real decision points, starting at week 3.
Quick wins built in+
Real progress visible by week 8–10, before the full recommendation lands.
Design Principles+
Continuity, Simplicity, Design for Reality, Near-term Impact, Value Creation, the five things this architecture has to hold true.
3

Roadmap and Implementation Plan

Included in the three phases
This is what Roadmap produces+
Rollout plan, member migration playbook, brand-aligned comms strategy, systems and data requirements, KPI framework.
Priced as one of the three phases+
Not an add-on. Planning how this gets built and rolled out is part of what's being proposed here.
Includes the pilot's design, where one is warranted+
Go/no-go thresholds, market selection criteria, control-group design, all part of this plan, if Design concludes a pilot makes sense.
